abstract: "Sensitivity analysis for imperfect instruments usually targets a linear two-stage least squares coefficient, yet many applications have binary outcomes and probability-scale targets. We extend the plausibly exogenous framework to the control-function probit, targeting average partial and interaction effects. We derive the generated-regressor variance, union and local-to-zero intervals, and their asymptotic coverage under stated model and calibration conditions. A violation stated on the wrong latent scale can miscalibrate the interval by a factor set by the endogeneity correlation. Applications to Catholic schooling, sibling sex composition and Confucian clans compare probit and linear analyses. Stata and R implementations accompany the analysis."
